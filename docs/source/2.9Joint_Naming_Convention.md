2.9 Joint Naming Convention
=======================

For ease of identification and debugging, this device adopts the following naming conventions:
- **Direction Abbreviations**
  * f = front
  * b = back
  * l = left
  * r = right
- **Number Meanings**
  * 1 = hip_roll
  * 2 = hip_pitch
  * 3 = knee_pitch
  * 4 = foot
- **Example Names**
  * fr1_hip_roll：Right front leg, joint No. 1 (hip roll)
  * bl3_knee_pitch：Left back leg, joint No. 3 (knee pitch)

```
front_left_leg      #Left front leg
front_right_leg    #Right front leg
back_left_leg       #Left back leg
back_right_leg     #Right back leg

fl1_hip_roll           #Left front, No. 1
fl2_hip_pitch        #Left front, No. 2
fl3_knee_pitch     #Left front, No. 3
fl4_foot                #Left front No. 4

fr1_hip_roll           #Right front, No. 1
fr2_hip_pitch        #Right front, No. 2
fr3_knee_pitch     #Right front, No. 3
fr4_foot                #Right front, No.4

bl1_hip_roll           #Left back, No. 1
bl2_hip_pitch        #Left back, No. 2
bl3_knee_pitch     #Left back, No. 3
bl4_foot                #Left back, No. 4

br1_hip_roll           #Right back, No. 1
br2_hip_pitch       #Right back, No. 2
br3_knee_pitch     #Right back, No. 3
br4_foot               #Right back, No. 4
```
