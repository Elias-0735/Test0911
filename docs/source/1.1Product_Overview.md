1.1 Product Overview
============

The AGIBOT D1 Max Quadruped Robot is a wheel‑legged bionic quadruped robot featuring low self‑weight, high payload capacity, long endurance, robust protection, and agile, stable locomotion. Each leg is equipped with 3 joint motors and 1 hub motor. The robot integrates optical cameras, LiDAR, ultrasonic sensors, an IMU, an RTK module, and other sensing devices. It is powered by a high‑performance onboard computing platform for motion control, autonomous navigation and positioning, environmental perception, and other functions. It provides extensive power and communication interfaces, supporting a wide range of task-payload expansions.
